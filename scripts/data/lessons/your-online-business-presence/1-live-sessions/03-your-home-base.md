# Your Home Base

> Build one simple page that says who you are, what you do, how to book, and where your reviews live, then use that one link everywhere.

## Why this matters

Your Google profile has a spot for a website. So do your social bios and your email signature. If each one points somewhere different, or nowhere, customers get lost. Take Denise, who runs a home daycare. Her Facebook page lists an old phone number, her Instagram has no link, and her Google profile has an empty website field. A parent ready to enroll has to hunt for how to reach her, and many will not. One page with the right information, linked from every place, fixes that in an afternoon.

## What you'll learn

- What a home base is and why you need only one
- Why a full website can wait
- The four things your page must say
- How to choose between a one-page site and a link page
- Where to put your one link once it exists

## The lesson

### One link for everything

A home base is a single page on the internet that you control and that every other profile points to. Think of it as your front door. Social media, Google, and email are the roads. All roads should lead to the same door.

Having one link matters for two reasons. First, the customer always lands in a place that is current. Second, when something changes, such as your phone number or your booking method, you fix it in one spot and every profile is correct again.

### A full website can wait

Many owners freeze here because they think they need a professional website with many pages, and that feels expensive and slow. You do not need that today. A customer deciding whether to call you wants four answers, and one page can give all four. Build the simple page now. If the business later needs online ordering, a gallery, or a blog, you can grow into a bigger site then.

### The four things your page must say

1. **Who you are.** Your business name, your name, a photo of you or your work, and the area you serve.
2. **What you do.** Three to six services in plain words. Not everything you could ever do, only what you want to be hired for.
3. **How to book.** One clear action: call, text, email, or fill out a form. Pick the one you will actually answer and make it the biggest thing on the page.
4. **Your reviews link.** A button or line that sends people to your Google reviews, so they can see proof and happy customers can add more.

Here is how a finished page reads for a fictional business.

> **Little Sprouts Home Daycare**
> Licensed home child care in Capitol Heights, run by Denise Walker.
>
> **What we offer**
> - Full-day care, ages 6 weeks to 4 years
> - Before- and after-school care
> - Meals and snacks included
> - Small group, never more than eight children
>
> **How to enroll**
> Call or text Denise to schedule a visit. Visits are held weekday evenings after 6.
>
> **What parents say**
> Read our Google reviews [reviews link]

That is the whole page. A parent can read it in thirty seconds and knows exactly what to do next.

### Pick your tool

You have three free options named for this session. All of them work on a phone and none requires design skill.

| Tool | Best when | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Sites | You want a simple page that looks like a small website | A page with sections, photos, and buttons, made with your Google account |
| Canva | You care about looks and like working from templates | A designed one-page site built from a template |
| A link-in-bio page | You mostly send people to other places | A short list of buttons: call, book, reviews, social |

If you are unsure, pick the one that sounds easiest to you. A finished plain page beats an unfinished beautiful one.

### Build it in numbered steps

1. Write your four sections in a notes app or on paper first. Words before design.
2. Open the tool you chose and start a new one-page site or link page.
3. Add your business name and one photo at the top.
4. Add your services as a short list.
5. Add your booking action as a button or a bold line with your phone number.
6. Add your reviews link.
7. Preview it on a phone, since that is how most customers will see it.
8. Publish, then copy the page link.

### Use it everywhere

Once you have the link, paste it into every place that asks for a website: your Google Business Profile, your LinkedIn contact info, each social media bio, and your email signature. Put it on your next batch of business cards and flyers. From now on, when someone asks "Do you have a website?" the answer is yes, and it is the same answer every time. One page, one link, used everywhere. Consistent and done.

## Do this now

1. Write your four sections: who, what, how to book, reviews.
2. Choose Google Sites, Canva, or a link-in-bio page.
3. Build the page with your name, photo, services, booking action, and reviews link.
4. Check it on your phone and have a neighbor read it. Ask them, "What would you do next?" If they cannot answer, make the booking action bigger.
5. Publish it and copy the link.
6. Paste the link into your Google Business Profile, your LinkedIn, and one social bio before the session ends.

Finished result: one live page and one link, already placed on at least three of your profiles.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting for a "real" website.** Fix: publish the one page now and improve it later.
- **Listing every service you have ever done.** Fix: keep it to the three to six you want more of.
- **Three ways to book and no clear favorite.** Fix: choose one main action and make it stand out.
- **Different links in different places.** Fix: replace them all with your one home base link.
- **Never checking it on a phone.** Fix: open your own link on your phone before you share it.

## Check yourself

- Can a stranger tell in thirty seconds what you do and how to hire you?
- Is there exactly one main booking action?
- Does the page link to your reviews?
- Where have you pasted the link so far, and where is it still missing?

## What you walk away with

A published one-page home base and a single link that now sits on your Google profile, your social bios, and soon your email signature.
